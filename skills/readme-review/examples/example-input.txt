# Kelp canopy survey, south Vancouver Island, 2022-2023

Collected by J. Moreau and A. Singh, Department of Biology, University of Victoria.
Questions: j.moreau@example.ca

Kelp canopy was measured by kayak at 14 sites between June and August of 2022 and 2023.
Canopy area was estimated by paddling the perimeter of each bed with a handheld GPS.

License: CC BY 4.0

## Files
kelp_sites.csv - one row per site visit.

## Variables
- site_id: site code (KS01 to KS14)
- lat, lon: site location (decimal degrees)
- date: visit date
- canopy_area: area of the kelp bed
