# Deposit checklist (starter version: replace with your institution's)

1. readme: A README explains what each file is, how the files relate, and how the data was produced.
2. data_dictionary: Every variable has a name, a definition, and units or allowed values.
3. file_formats: Files are in open or widely readable formats (CSV over XLSX, PDF/A or TXT over DOCX, etc.), or a reason is given.
4. file_naming: File names are consistent and meaningful, with no spaces or special characters.
5. sensitive_data: The material says whether the data involves human participants and, if so, how identifiable information was removed or protected and what participants consented to.
6. indigenous_data: If the data involves Indigenous communities, lands, or knowledge, the material addresses community rights and governance (e.g. CARE principles, OCAP).
7. license: A license or terms of use is stated.
8. methods: Collection methods are described clearly enough for someone to understand how the data was produced.
9. funder: Funding is acknowledged (agency and grant number) if applicable.
