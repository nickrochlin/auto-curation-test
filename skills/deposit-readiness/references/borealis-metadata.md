# Borealis citation metadata (starter reference)

Required fields in Borealis (Dataverse citation block):
- title: A specific, descriptive title. Include topic, place, and time period where relevant.
- author: Name(s) and affiliation(s) of the people who created the data.
- contact: A point of contact (name; an email is required at deposit time).
- description: An abstract of the dataset: what it contains, how and why it was collected, and the time period and place covered.
- subject: One or more values from this list ONLY:
  - Agricultural Sciences
  - Arts and Humanities
  - Astronomy and Astrophysics
  - Business and Management
  - Chemistry
  - Computer and Information Science
  - Earth and Environmental Sciences
  - Engineering
  - Law
  - Mathematical Sciences
  - Medicine, Health and Life Sciences
  - Physics
  - Social Sciences
  - Other

Strongly recommended:
- keywords: 3 to 8 specific terms that someone searching would use.
- kind_of_data: e.g. survey data, interview transcripts, geospatial data, images.
- time_period: The period the data covers (not when it was deposited).
- geographic_coverage: Places the data is about.
- funder: Funding agency and grant number, if given.

Borealis supports English and French metadata.
